TerraSora Tracker (TST)

A TerraSora Learn project.

Status: In development / testing.

TerraSora Tracker helps sellers track deals and follow-ups directly inside messaging apps. Sellers message TST to create, update, and close deals — and receive reminders automatically.

TST only processes messages sent directly to it and does not monitor private seller-customer conversations.
