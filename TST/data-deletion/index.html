User Data Deletion

TerraSora Tracker (TST) is a TerraSora Learn project. Status: in development / testing.

You can request deletion of your TST data at any time.

How to request deletion

Email us at al.zol@terrasora.com with:

1) Subject line: TST Data Deletion Request
2) The messaging channel you used (WhatsApp or Instagram)
3) Your account identifier (phone number for WhatsApp, or your Instagram profile link/handle)
4) Optional: “Delete all data” or specify a subset (e.g., “Delete deal history only”)

What we delete

We will delete message content you sent directly to TST and the associated deal/reminder records stored for your account, except where retention is required for security, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

Timing

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.
